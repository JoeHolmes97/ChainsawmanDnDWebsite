The world of tabletop roleplaying games (TTRPGs) has boomed in the public consciousness of late. With the massive success of roleplaying groups such as Critical Role and 
Dimension 20, the demand for immersive storytelling is at its peak and the scene is set for innovative groups of creative nerds (I say this with love) to share their own tabletop 
roleplaying experiences. Homebrew (game material created by players of a game) content allows for the use of your own ideas and rules within the mechanics of a TTRPG campaign, 
and offers experiences that you can’t get anywhere else (at least to begin with). Enter: Dungeon Delinquents.

Dungeon Delinquents aim to shock and entertain with Devil World, their new Chainsaw Man D&D podcast, inspired by the anime of the same name. With professional actors 
Gigi Platt (playing Alina Starling and Miss Fortune), Matheiu Wallies (playing Crash), Jermaine Green (playing Hakeem Jahar) and the wonderful 
John Barbour (as Miss Fortune and the Dungeon Master) taking the stage, expect devil-fuelled chaos and explosive laughs.

Dungeon Delinquents’ debut campaign, Devil World follows Preliminary Squad 5 - a group of low-ranking Devil Hunters known as Unlisters - in the throes of a war-torn world rife 
with conflict between humans and devils. In the aftermath of destruction, the U.K population has been decimated and the Royal Family is dead. The Royal Devil Hunters (RDH) 
association has been created in an effort to push back the onslaught of devils in the land of New United Kingdom (N.U.K.), and our characters want a piece of the world-saving pie.

With the massive fanbase of animes such as Chainsaw Man and the crossover with fans of Dungeons & Dragons and other RPGs, Dungeon Delinquents provides a podcast listening 
experience that you just can’t find anywhere else. The dark modern fantasy world of Chainsaw Man lends itself beautifully to the exploration of good and evil, what it means to 
be controlled, and the consequences of manipulation and making a deal with the devil. And with our colourful cast of characters trying their best to make it past probation, 
they’ll have to be exceptional if they hope to make any sort of impact on the world.
