Welcome to the first official Chainsaw Man DND session! 
This is a 5e dungeons and dragons game with a spattering 
of homebrew. This game is set in the new united kingdom 
a post-apocalyptic England ripped apart by The Great Devil
War. We follow the three main characters Hakeem Jahar
(Jermaine), Alina Starling (Gigi) and Crash (Matt) on
their adventures to destroy devils and climb the 
hierarchy of the RDH (Royal Devil Hunters) 
DMed by me (John). 


Session two sees the team try to piece together the
location of this serial killer making sure to spend
some time enjoying a local café on their break.
